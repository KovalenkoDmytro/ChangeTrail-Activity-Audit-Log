# ChangeTrail – Activity & Audit Log

ChangeTrail is a free WordPress plugin that keeps a private log of important changes on your site. It shows you who changed what and when, so you always know what happened on your site, even when several people work on it.

## Who it's for

- **Site owners** who want to know what changes are being made on their site.
- **Agencies and freelancers** who manage client sites and need a clear history of client and team activity.

## What it does

- Records logins, logouts and failed login attempts.
- Records new and deleted users, and role, email and password changes.
- Records when posts and pages are created, edited, trashed, restored or deleted, with a clear before-and-after view.
- Records plugin and theme installs, updates, activations and deletions, and WordPress updates.
- Records changes to key site settings, such as the site address or admin email.
- Shows which computer and browser did it on every entry (device cookie, login session, browser and an optional device fingerprint), so people sharing one account and one office IP can be told apart. Devices can be named in the **Devices** tab.
- Shows everything under **Tools > ChangeTrail** in a list that looks like the rest of WordPress, with search and filters.
- Only the site owner can open the log, and you can add an extra password.
- Lets you choose how long to keep entries, export them to CSV, or delete them.

## Privacy

ChangeTrail works fully inside your WordPress site. It doesn't need an account and doesn't send your data to any outside service. It works with the WordPress personal data export and erase tools. To tell devices apart it stores a device cookie, a short hash of the login session, the browser user agent and (unless turned off in Settings) a few device details such as screen size, graphics card and timezone; all of this is listed in the suggested privacy policy text.

## Where to get it

- **WordPress.org:** [wordpress.org/plugins/kovalenko-admin-change-audit](https://wordpress.org/plugins/kovalenko-admin-change-audit/). You can also install it right from your dashboard: go to **Plugins > Add New** and search for "ChangeTrail".
- **GitHub:** [github.com/KovalenkoDmytro/ChangeTrail-Activity-Audit-Log](https://github.com/KovalenkoDmytro/ChangeTrail-Activity-Audit-Log)

## Installation

Install and activate ChangeTrail from **Plugins > Add New**, then open **Tools > ChangeTrail** to see your activity log.
